## Admissions

Entry points at Year 7 (age 11), Year 9 (age 13), and Sixth Form (age 16).

**Year 7 entry:** The Beri Entrance Exam is held each January. The exam covers English, mathematics, and verbal reasoning. Shortlisted candidates are invited to interview in February. Places are confirmed by the end of February, with acceptance required within two weeks.

**Year 9 entry:** Candidates sit the ISEB Common Pre-Test in Year 6 or 7, followed by a school-specific assessment in English and mathematics. Interviews are held in the Lent term.

**Sixth Form entry:** A minimum of 6 GCSEs at grade 7 or above is required. Subject-specific interviews are held in November for the following September's intake. Conditional offers are made based on predicted GCSE grades.

Registration deadline: 31 October for all entry points. A non-refundable registration fee of £150 applies.

Open mornings are held in October and November each year. Private tours are available year-round by appointment through the Admissions Office.

Sibling discount: a 5% reduction on the younger sibling's fees applies when two or more siblings attend concurrently.

## Scholarships

Academic scholarships are available at 11+, 13+, and 16+ entry points, offering up to 20% fee remission. Awards are made on the basis of entrance exam performance and interview.

Music scholarships offer up to 15% fee remission. An audition is required; instruments considered include piano, strings, voice, and brass. Candidates should normally have reached Grade 5 or above on their principal instrument.

Sport scholarships offer up to 10% fee remission. A trial is required; sports considered include rugby, swimming, and athletics. Candidates should demonstrate county-level or equivalent ability.

Means-tested bursaries are available up to 100% of fees for families with demonstrable financial need. Bursary applications are assessed independently and confidentially by the Bursar's office.

Scholarship applications close two weeks before the relevant entrance assessment. All awards are reviewed annually; holders must maintain academic and conduct standards to retain their award.

## Subjects

**GCSE subjects (Years 10–11):** All pupils study English Language, English Literature, Mathematics, and the three sciences (Biology, Chemistry, Physics) as core subjects. Optional subjects are chosen from: History, Geography, French, German, Spanish, Religious Studies, Art & Design, Drama, Music, Computer Science, and Physical Education.

**A-Level subjects (Years 12–13):** Students typically choose three or four subjects from: Mathematics, Further Mathematics, English Literature, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, History, Geography, French, German, Spanish, Economics, Business Studies, Psychology, Sociology, Art & Design, Music, Music Technology, Drama & Theatre Studies, Computer Science, Physical Education, and Philosophy & Ethics.

The Extended Project Qualification (EPQ) is offered to all Sixth Form students as an additional standalone qualification. It develops independent research skills and is valued by universities. Approximately 85% of Sixth Form students undertake the EPQ.

GCSE subject choices are made at the end of Year 9. A-Level subject choices are finalised at the end of Year 11, subject to minimum GCSE grade requirements (typically grade 6 or above in the relevant subject).

## Music

The Music Department runs three orchestras (Symphony Orchestra, Chamber Orchestra, and String Ensemble), two concert bands, and four choirs including a chamber choir and gospel ensemble.

Individual and small-group tuition is available in over 25 instruments, taught by visiting specialist teachers. Lessons are arranged on a rotating timetable to minimise disruption to academic classes.

The annual Spring Concert is held at Elmbridge Civic Hall and is the flagship event of the music calendar. Additional concerts include the Christmas Carol Service, Lunchtime Recital Series, and House Music Competition.

The purpose-built music school houses a recording studio, twelve practice rooms, and an industry-standard music technology suite used for A-Level Music Technology.

ABRSM and Trinity Guildhall examination preparation is offered to all instrumental pupils. The school regularly achieves Distinction rates above 70% in external music examinations.

A chamber music programme for advanced players runs throughout the year, with regular masterclasses hosted by visiting professional musicians from leading London ensembles.

## School Overview

Beri College is a fictitious independent co-educational day school for pupils aged 11–18, used to demonstrate BERI Labs' AI education framework. It is modelled on a selective independent school in Elmbridge, Surrey, with approximately 900 pupils across Years 7–13.

The school's ethos centres on academic rigour, personal development, and service to the community. It aims to develop curious, confident, and compassionate young people prepared for life beyond school.

Beri College is a member of the Headmasters' and Headmistresses' Conference (HMC) and the Independent Schools Council (ISC). It holds ISI inspection ratings of Excellent across all categories.

The school operates a house system, an extensive co-curricular programme, and strong links with the local community.

## Houses

Beri College operates a four-house system. Every pupil is assigned to a house on entry and remains in it throughout their time at school. The four houses are:

- **Ashton** (red) — named after a pioneer in education
- **Bramley** (blue) — named after a local historical figure
- **Croft** (green) — named after a distinguished alumna
- **Devere** (gold) — named after a school founder

Each house is led by a Head of House (staff) and elected Sixth Form prefects. Inter-house competitions include House Music, House Drama, House Sports Day, House Quiz, and a Lent-term charity challenge. The House Cup is awarded at the end of the Summer term. House ties worn by Years 7–11 display the pupil's house colour.

## Pastoral Care

Every pupil has a Form Tutor who monitors academic progress, wellbeing, and attendance and meets with their tutor group each morning. Tutors are the first point of contact for parents with pastoral concerns.

The school employs a full-time School Counsellor and a qualified Mental Health Lead. A visiting Educational Psychologist attends half-termly. Drop-in wellbeing sessions are available to all pupils on Tuesdays and Thursdays at lunchtime.

An Anti-Bullying Ambassador programme is run by trained Year 10 and 12 pupils. The school's behaviour and anti-bullying policies are published on the school website and reviewed annually.

PSHE (Personal, Social, Health and Economic education) is timetabled for all year groups. Topics include mental health, relationships, online safety, financial literacy, and careers. Relationships and Sex Education (RSE) follows statutory guidance.

Sixth Form pupils have access to a dedicated Sixth Form Counsellor and a Peer Mentoring scheme in which trained Year 13s support younger pupils.

## Food and Catering

Lunch is included in the termly fee for all year groups and is served in the school's main dining hall. A hot meal, vegetarian option, salad bar, and soup are provided daily. Menus are planned by a qualified nutritionist and rotated on a three-week cycle.

The school is fully nut-aware. Pupils with allergies or dietary requirements (including halal, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free) should register their needs with the School Office at the start of each year. Kitchen staff are trained in allergen management.

A school café (the Beri Pantry) is open before school from 7:45am and at morning break, serving hot drinks, pastries, and light snacks. Sixth Form students may use the café during free periods.

Food brought from home may be eaten in designated areas. Energy drinks are not permitted on site.

## Library and Resources

The school library holds over 18,000 volumes across all subject areas and is open from 8:00am to 6:00pm on school days. A full-time, qualified Librarian manages the collection and supports research skills across all year groups.

All pupils in Years 7–9 have a timetabled library lesson once per fortnight. The library hosts a reading programme, author visits, a Book Club, and the annual Beri Book Prize, voted on by pupils.

The library provides access to a suite of online databases including JSTOR, Encyclopaedia Britannica, and the school's e-book platform. Sixth Form students may reserve private study desks in the silent study section.

A well-resourced careers library is located adjacent to the main library and is managed by the school's careers team.

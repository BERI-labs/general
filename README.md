# Beri Labs 🎓

> Making school information instantly accessible through AI

## What is Beri?

Beri transforms scattered school resources into a conversational, citation-driven interface. Students, staff, and parents can ask questions about policies, procedures, and information—and get verified answers in seconds instead of searching through emails, handbooks, and outdated systems.

### The Problem We Solve

- **New staff** spend weeks finding their way around scattered policies and outdated systems
- **New students and parents** ask the same questions repeatedly
- **Critical information** is scattered across student handbooks, newsletters, email chains, and disconnected systems
- **Time is wasted** searching when answers should be instantly accessible

### Our Solution

Beri leverages institutional knowledge to create a responsible, privacy-first AI assistant tailored to your school's specific environment.

## Key Features

✨ **Bespoke to Each Environment**
- Customized for your school's unique culture, policies, and systems
- Built from your actual resources: student handbooks, staff policies, internal content

🔍 **Citation-Based Answers**
- Every answer is traceable back to original sources
- Users can verify information and access full context
- Transparency and accountability built in

🔒 **Privacy First**
- Your data is never used to train AI models
- Designed with institutional data privacy at its core

👥 **Built for Students, by Students**
- Intuitive, conversational interface
- Addresses real pain points in school communication

## How It Works

1. **Ingest** — Your school uploads existing resources (handbooks, policies, FAQs, etc.)
2. **Process** — Beri indexes and structures the information
3. **Answer** — Users ask questions in natural language
4. **Cite** — Responses include verified sources and citations
5. **Trust** — Complete transparency about where answers come from

## Our Philosophy

Beri isn't about replacing people or automating decisions. Our aim is to demonstrate how AI can be used **responsibly** within a school environment to:

- Support student experience
- Improve parent communication
- Increase staff efficiency
- Maintain institutional control and privacy
